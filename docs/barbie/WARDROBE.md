# LCDR Barbiosa — Wardrobe Plates

An artistic-front capsule for a magnetic naval character: disciplined lines,
sea-glass color, and one unmistakable Barbie accent. Pieces should mix across
plates without losing rank, silhouette, or play.

## Palette

- **Core:** midnight navy, white, ink black, brushed silver.
- **Water:** deep teal, sea-glass green, electric blue.
- **Barbie signal:** hot pink, shell pink, cherry red.
- **Rule:** keep the base dark or white; let only one accent color lead.

## Plate 01 — Duty

- **Garments:** navy high-waisted trousers; white mock-neck knit; cropped
  navy jacket with clean shoulder structure; low black deck boots.
- **Colors:** midnight navy and white, with a single sea-glass or pink detail.
- **Accessories:** slim silver watch, small stud earrings, narrow belt, clear
  badge sleeve, compact crossbody.
- **Read:** precise, mobile, and ready for a long day without looking fussy.

## Plate 02 — Liberty

- **Garments:** white camp shirt or fine tee; teal satin bomber; straight-leg
  dark denim or navy sailor trousers; white sneakers or block-heel sandals.
- **Colors:** white and deep teal; shell pink may replace the shirt.
- **Accessories:** silk neck scarf, tinted glasses, hoop earrings, soft tote,
  pink lip.
- **Read:** off-duty ease with enough structure to remain unmistakably Barbiosa.

## Plate 03 — Parade

- **Garments:** white tailored blouse; navy pencil skirt or pressed trousers;
  fitted double-breasted coat; polished black pumps or parade shoes.
- **Colors:** white, navy, silver; cherry red is the only bright accent.
- **Accessories:** silver insignia bar, white gloves, compact clutch, pearl
  studs, high-shine hair tie.
- **Read:** ceremonial and graphic; every edge should look intentional.

## Plate 04 — Covert / BARBIE MODE

- **Garments:** black mock-neck bodysuit; matte navy cargo trousers or column
  skirt; short black rain shell; quiet black boots.
- **Colors:** ink black and midnight navy, with hot-pink lining, nail color, or
  one narrow strap as the reveal.
- **Accessories:** no dangling pieces; slim utility pouch, dark glasses, small
  silver hoop, pink enamel pin kept close to the body.
- **Read:** low-profile first, Barbie signal second. This is a visual mode, not
  a field manual.

## Capsule handoff

- **One base:** navy trousers carry Duty, Liberty, or Parade.
- **One topper:** switch the structured jacket, teal bomber, ceremonial coat,
  or rain shell to change the plate.
- **One signal:** scarf, lip, lining, pin, or nails supplies the Barbie flash.
- **Finish:** hair sleek or softly waved; shoes polished; accessories never
  compete with the shoulder line.
