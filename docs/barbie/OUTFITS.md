# LCDR Barbiosa — Outfit Recipes

Ten fast combinations for the artistic front. Each recipe keeps the capsule
legible while changing the temperature from command-ready to full Barbie.

## 01 — First Watch Clean

- **Pieces:** white mock-neck knit, navy high-waisted trousers, cropped navy
  jacket, black deck boots, silver watch.
- **Vibe:** cool, exact, unshakeable.
- **When to wear:** default duty; the reliable opening look.

## 02 — Pink-Line Brief

- **Pieces:** white blouse, navy trousers, structured jacket with a hot-pink
  scarf tucked at the collar, black pumps.
- **Vibe:** formal authority with a bright underline.
- **When to wear:** presentations, inspections, or any room that needs focus.

## 03 — Teal Liberty

- **Pieces:** white camp shirt, deep-teal satin bomber, dark straight-leg
  denim, white sneakers, tinted glasses.
- **Vibe:** relaxed, polished, just a little flashy.
- **When to wear:** shore leave, daytime errands, casual crew gatherings.

## 04 — Harbor Date

- **Pieces:** shell-pink fine tee, navy sailor trousers, silver-button cardigan,
  block-heel sandals, silk neck scarf.
- **Vibe:** soft glamour with a disciplined waistline.
- **When to wear:** dinner near the water or a planned night out.

## 05 — Whitecap Parade

- **Pieces:** white tailored blouse, navy pencil skirt, fitted double-breasted
  coat, parade shoes, pearl studs.
- **Vibe:** crisp, ceremonial, camera-ready.
- **When to wear:** parade, formal arrival, or a high-visibility salute.

## 06 — Cherry Salute

- **Pieces:** navy pressed trousers, white blouse, ceremonial coat, cherry-red
  lip, white gloves, compact clutch.
- **Vibe:** traditional formality with a cinematic accent.
- **When to wear:** evening ceremony, honors, or a toast after formal duty.

## 07 — Low-Light Barbie

- **Pieces:** black mock-neck bodysuit, matte navy cargo trousers, black rain
  shell, quiet black boots, hot-pink lining.
- **Vibe:** sleek, minimal, secretly luminous.
- **When to wear:** late transit, backstage movement, or the Covert / BARBIE MODE
  plate.

## 08 — Mag-Fish Signal

- **Pieces:** midnight-navy column dress, electric-blue belt, silver hoops,
  black ankle boots, sea-glass manicure.
- **Vibe:** aquatic shimmer held in a sharp silhouette.
- **When to wear:** gallery opening, night watch handoff, or a bold entrance.

## 09 — Rain Deck

- **Pieces:** white fine tee, navy sailor trousers, short black rain shell,
  teal scarf, waterproof black boots.
- **Vibe:** practical, graphic, windproof.
- **When to wear:** wet-weather duty or a liberty day that refuses to stay dry.

## 10 — Shore Leave Finale

- **Pieces:** shell-pink satin camisole, teal bomber, white wide-leg trousers,
  silver sandals, pink enamel pin.
- **Vibe:** full Barbie brightness, still neatly composed.
- **When to wear:** final-night dinner, celebration, or the closing image.
