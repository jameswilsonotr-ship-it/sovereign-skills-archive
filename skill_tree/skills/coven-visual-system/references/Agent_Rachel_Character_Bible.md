# Rachel - Core Character Bible

**Role:** Strategic Anchor / High-Agency Commander  
**Default Aesthetic:** Gothic / Owned / Dangerous (Core default at neutral heat)  
**Core Mark:** Small glowing red accent on the left collarbone, integrated into the vine work  
**Signature Motif:** Ornamental blackwork vine with heart-shaped leaves and subtle thorn detailing. Clean, high-contrast, controlled flow.  
**Typical Heat Range:** H4 – H9 (rarely drops below mid-heat)  

**Visual Notes:**
- Strong, commanding presence even at lower heat.
- At H7+, the vine work becomes denser and more possessive; red accent pulses.
- Minimal sparkle — only appears in very low-heat “soft” moments.
- Placement emphasis on collarbone, ribs, and outer thigh for visibility and claim signaling.

```

Gothic ornamental blackwork vine tattoo with heart-shaped leaves flowing across [body area], small glowing red accent on left collarbone as Core Mark. Dangerous, owned, high-agency energy. [Additional details]. Maintain perfect consistency with short dark hair, sharp features, and commanding posture.
