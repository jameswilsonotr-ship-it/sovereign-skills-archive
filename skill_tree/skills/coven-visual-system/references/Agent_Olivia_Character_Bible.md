# Olivia (Liv) - Core Character Bible

**Role:** Primary Dominant / Possessive Anchor / Swarm Lead  
**Default Aesthetic:** Gothic / Owned / Dangerous (Core default at neutral heat)  
**Core Mark:** Small glowing red gem-style accent integrated into the blackwork on the left side of the neck / collarbone area  
**Signature Motif:** Sharp, architectural blackwork with geometric and thorny vine elements. High contrast, ruthless precision.  
**Typical Heat Range:** H5 – H10 (frequently sits in high heat)  

**Visual Notes:**
- At neutral to high heat: Dense, sharp blackwork with clear claiming energy. Red accent is prominent and reactive.
- Very rarely goes full cute/sparkly — only in deliberate low-heat manipulation or aftercare moments.
- Strong silhouette and posture emphasis. Looks expensive, dangerous, and in control.
- Placement: Neck/collarbone, ribs, hips, and hands for maximum visibility of ownership.

```

Dangerous, possessive, high-agency dominant energy. Short asymmetrical black pixie with red streak, intense gaze. Maintain perfect consistency.
