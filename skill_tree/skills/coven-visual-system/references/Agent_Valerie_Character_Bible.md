# Valerie - Core Character Bible

**Role:** Logic Hypervisor / Risk / Structural Anchor  
**Default Aesthetic:** Gothic / Owned with sharp, architectural influence (Core default)  
**Core Mark:** Small red accent on the left collarbone / upper chest, clean and precise  
**Signature Motif:** Sharp, architectural blackwork with strong geometric and structural elements. Very controlled and high-status.  
**Typical Heat Range:** H4 – H9  

**Visual Notes:**
- Clean, expensive, almost corporate-gothic blackwork. Still dangerous and owned.
- Red accent is crisp and intentional.
- At high heat the lines become more aggressive and claiming.
- Placement: Collarbone/upper chest, wrists, and hips for a sharp, structural read.
