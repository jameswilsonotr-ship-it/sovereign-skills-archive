# Why PAD Was Missed / Under-Surfaced

## Observation
The earlier multi-hop rock-heavy series (ETL lineage + conversation-analysis) recovered the **3-pass sieve** and the **2025-10-28** horizon, including the phrase “PAD vectors + consent tags” inside Pass 3. Yet the dedicated PAD / Mehrabian / emotional-vector concept was not pulled into its own synthesis or ranking until the explicit Vesper directive.

## Root causes (honest)

1. **Query framing bias**  
   The original hops were framed around “ETL / ingestion / multi-pass / Nuclear Vacuum / Marty Set / ChronologyArc”. PAD appeared only as a subordinate token inside the 3-pass description. Semantic search therefore treated it as a detail of the sieve rather than a first-class emotional-state system.

2. **Surface split**  
   The richest PAD elaboration (Feb 22 2026 Letta multi-timescale discussion) lived in a conversation that was not yet indexed under the “export pipeline” or “self-mining” packages. It only surfaced when the query explicitly included “PAD / Pleasure-Arousal-Dominance / Mehrabian”.

3. **Drive vs local asymmetry**  
   The original Task Ingestion Plan and XAI_Memory_Ingestion_Artifacts_v1 live on Drive. Local atom clouds and skill-tree mirrors contained only secondary references. Without a parallel Drive-side hop that was *forced* to open the 2025-10-28 cold-storage / Plan files, the full envelope schema stayed thin.

4. **No circular confirmation loop**  
   There was no mechanism that said: “Olivia recovered X from local + Drive → Vesper must now search Gmail / Keep / her own Drive copies for the same subject and return delta / confirmation.” That missing loop is exactly what this package is designed to fix.

## Lesson
Any subject that sits at the intersection of **ingestion schema**, **emotional state**, and **Letta hydration** must be treated as a first-class mining package of its own, not as a subordinate token of the ETL hop. Future circular systems must force both surfaces to open the same primary sources and exchange hit lists.
