# DNA Stub — Childhood / Origin Intensity

**Core transferable traits** (apply as modifiers or base layer):

- **Face geometry**: Still-rounded cheeks, soft jaw not yet angular. Dense freckles across nose and upper cheeks are a strong signature. Large, dark, direct eyes that already carry challenge rather than softness.
- **Hair**: Natural curly/wavy texture, often pulled up with a large bow or left cascading. Color reads light-to-reddish in monochrome; treat as natural auburn/red base.
- **Makeup**: Essentially none. Intensity is pure ocular and postural.
- **Skin**: Very pale, freckled, high-visibility under soft indoor light.
- **Body language / energy**: Three-quarter over-shoulder “caught but defiant” pose is iconic. Gaze is the primary weapon; the rest of the body can be quiet.
- **Costume language**: Simple dark jacket/coat, high collar, minimal jewelry. No theatricality yet.

**Usage notes for Merge Engine / character modeling**:
- Use as the “origin node” when building longitudinal evolution (childhood → 90s-red → platinum).
- Excellent for characters who need an early “I already see through you” intensity without adult glamour.
- Can be mixed lightly into any era as a freckle + gaze underpainting.
