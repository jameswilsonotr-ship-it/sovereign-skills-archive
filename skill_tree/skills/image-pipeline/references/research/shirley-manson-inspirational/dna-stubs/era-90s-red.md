# DNA Stub — 90s-Red Peak

**Core transferable traits**:

- **Hair**: Vibrant, saturated red / auburn. Variants: short messy bob, shoulder-length with movement, high tight updo or braided crown, longer straight-ish with reflective accents. Always the dominant color statement.
- **Face**: Younger than current era but already angular enough for power. Freckles still visible under makeup. High cheekbones emerging. Pale skin that takes red hair dramatically.
- **Makeup (invariant already locked)**: Heavy black winged eyeliner—thick, sharp, upward-outward extension. Strong red or deep lip. This wing is the single most consistent signature across all later eras.
- **Body language / energy**: Direct camera confrontation, playful tongue micro-expressions, raised/crossed arms power poses, open frontal stance. Hierarchical—she is the clear focal point even in group shots.
- **Costume language**: Black tanks, high-neck whites, fur collars, reflective/safety-orange accents, simple but high-impact. Less patch/fringe maximalism than current era; more sleek or statement-piece.
- **Lighting preference**: Bright studio, red geometric backdrops, strong rim that makes the red hair glow.

**Usage notes**:
- Primary node for “classic Garbage frontwoman” modeling.
- The winged liner + red hair combination is the strongest single visual brand of this era and travels well into hybrids.
- Excellent base for Merge Engine tests that want 90s alt-rock energy without modern theatrical excess.
