# DNA Stub — Current Platinum

**Core transferable traits**:

- **Hair**: Platinum / white-blonde. Variants: tightly scraped back into knot or high pony/braid (exposing forehead and bone structure), long loose waves, high sleek ponytail. Occasional multi-color yarn/fringe extensions near the hairline.
- **Face**: Fully mature and angular—high cheekbones, defined jaw, slight under-eye hollowing that reads as lived-in intensity rather than softness. Fair skin with stage texture.
- **Makeup (invariant)**: Extremely heavy, graphic black winged liner (calligraphic, long extension). Strong red or deep lip. The wing is now theatrical and almost architectural.
- **Body language / energy**: Athletic and high-motion—raised legs, wide arm gestures, hip cock, open chest, full-commitment screams or sustained notes. Controlled intensity rather than chaos. Political messaging is frequently embodied in clothing.
- **Costume language (highly distinctive)**: 
  - Graphic appliqué patches (stylized masks/faces, stars, Korean text, symbols)
  - Long multi-color or red fringe on jackets, sleeves, hems
  - Harness straps, belts, structured overalls or jumpsuits
  - Statement political tees (“STOP KILLING CHILDREN”)
  - Occasional high-fashion editorial (sequined wide-leg, pink tulle)
  - Heavy stacked silver bracelets, rings, earpieces
- **Lighting preference**: Strong colored stage washes (red, blue, magenta), rim light on platinum hair, high-contrast theatrical.

**Usage notes**:
- This is the richest and most “current” node for Merge Engine work.
- The combination of extreme wing + platinum + fringe/patch vocabulary is highly specific and travels well as an extension onto other base characters.
- Political messaging and athletic stage presence are optional but powerful secondary layers.
