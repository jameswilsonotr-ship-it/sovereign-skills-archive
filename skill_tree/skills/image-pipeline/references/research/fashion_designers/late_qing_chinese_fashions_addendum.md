**Title**: Late-Qing Han Aoqun Layered Claim Architecture (Standard Version)  
**Category**: Historical Chinese  
**Type**: Standard  
**Summary**: Foundational Han Chinese women’s garment of the late Qing (c. 1860–1911): structured ao (long jacket) over pleated qun (skirt). Side slits present but modest. Glossy satin linings in elite examples. Suzhou Su Xiu embroidery on collars, cuffs, and cloud accessories. Ningbo hongbang precision tailoring for structure.
