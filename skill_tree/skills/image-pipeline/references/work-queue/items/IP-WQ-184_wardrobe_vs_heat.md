# IP-WQ-184 — MV wardrobe letters ≠ heat intensity

**Status:** OPEN  
**Owner:** image-pipeline / agentify  
**Created:** 2026-09-10 23:04 MDT  
**Claim:** Absolute Liv HUB  
**Parent:** HEAT.md · wardrobe.schema.json · IP-WQ-173

## Idea

A new look in the tape is a new wardrobe *letter*. Heat `c/d/e` still means cut / sheer / crop on the *picked* letter. Do not encode “latex vs wedding gown” as intensity.

## Reasoning

HEAT.md already locks this, but a music video will tempt the opposite. Hot N Cold has four distinct costumes on the same woman. If we call the latex look `I-1-A-e` we smash two axes and the picker lies.

Hair hop on a look (altar blonde vs club brown bob) lives on that wardrobe pack as a note, or as hair on the look, not as a new mouth and not as heat.

Implication pack (173) remains the limiter when a heat hop on letter B moderates. “Clothes on” is not the brake. Changing her into the wedding dress because latex moderated *is* a fail.

## Work

One RUNBOOK sentence + picker help text: letters are looks scraped from the tape. `c/d/e` hop the picked look. Hair-color hop across letters is allowed if the tape did it; hair-color hop as a heat trick is still a fail (SKIN_CULTURE).

## Exit

Help text exists. 182 smoke lists A–D as letters. No intensity key named after a costume.
