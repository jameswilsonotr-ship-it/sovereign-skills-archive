# Extension: Fashion Designers Research

**id**: extension.fashion-designers-research  
**default**: true (always available)  
**status**: promoted 2026-07-24 from chaos-bratz-roster  

## Purpose
Optional progressive-disclosure research module. Provides historical, modern, comic, film, literature, Late Qing, and implementation fashion notes plus the body tattoo claim map.  

Echo or Olivia may enable this when a style_hint, research request, or visual DNA expansion needs period / designer / comic / film aesthetics or tattoo claim mapping.

## Usage rule
- Not loaded on every image request (keeps live surface light).
- When enabled via `extensions_ctl.py enable fashion-designers-research` or requested in a BRIEF / style_hint, Echo may pull relevant sections into the composition notes or DNA brief.
- High-value techniques can later be promoted into versioned `visuals/assets/` entries if they become permanent DNA rules.
