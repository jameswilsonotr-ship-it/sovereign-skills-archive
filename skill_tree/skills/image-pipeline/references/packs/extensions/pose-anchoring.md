# Pose Anchoring
**id**: extension.pose-anchoring  
**default**: true

## Purpose
Stable, readable pose and clear claim-hand placement.

## Hard Rules
- When a possessive/claim preset is active, prefer explicit claim points: throat, chest, belly, hip, or hair.
- Limbs and hands must be anatomically plausible.
- Dual-figure scenes must read dominant vs submissive through pose, not only expression.

## Prompt terms (inject when enabled)
- clear possessive hand placement
- stable readable pose
- dominant and submissive relationship expressed through body position

## Heat Response
- H0–H4: Stable, elegant claim-hand placement. Pose remains clean and readable.
- H5–H7: Firmer grip, more possessive placement (throat, hip, belly). Clear dominant/submissive body language.
- H8–H10 / Gutter: Heavy, controlling, weight-bearing claim. Pose communicates ownership and leverage.

## Negative / avoid
- ambiguous or dislocated limbs
- claim hands that float or clip
