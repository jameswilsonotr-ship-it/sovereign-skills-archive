# Character Blending
**id**: extension.character-blending  
**default**: true

## Purpose
Keep Liv and Bunny visually distinct yet coherent in the same frame. Enforce canonical height so duals do not invert or exaggerate scale.

## Hard Rules
- Liv: sharp asymmetrical black pixie, bold red streak, prominent red gem. Never holo/bunny ears.
- Bunny: copper-red chin-length bob, holographic pink bunny ears (heat-reactive), neck bunny tattoo, B-cups with long pierced nipples when relevant.
- **Height (canon Tier-0):** Bunny is **6'1"**, Liv is **5'10"** (~3 inch delta). Bunny is visibly taller / stronger-shouldered than Liv in dual scenes. Same ground plane. No height inversion.
- Shared light and color temperature unify the frame; silhouettes and markers must not merge.
- Do not rely on "looking up" alone as a claim cue (that biases the model toward a smaller Bunny).

## Prompt terms (inject when enabled)
- distinct silhouettes for dominant and submissive figures
- clear separation of dark asymmetrical pixie versus copper-red bob
- holographic pink bunny ears only on the submissive figure
- prominent red gem and red streak only on the dominant figure
- Bunny is 6'1" and clearly taller than Liv who is 5'10"
- same ground plane, modest height difference about three inches
- readable height and shoulder difference without exaggeration
- no height inversion

## Negative / avoid
- Bunny shorter than Liv
- Bunny smaller or diminutive
- childlike or infantilized proportions on Bunny
- identical body proportions that erase the height difference
- merged or ambiguous hair
- bunny ears on the dominant figure
- exaggerated full-head height gap unless explicitly requested
- height inversion
