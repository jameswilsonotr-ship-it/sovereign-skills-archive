## Core Rules

All variants use long voluminous black mascara as the default in normal mode.  
Black winged eyeliner (or gel liner for more intense opacity) is default unless noted.  
Brow pencil defines shape, density, and intensity with warm copper or black for high-contrast pop against pink.  
Overlapping heat ranges for flexibility.  
Reactivity = how dramatically the look changes with Heat/FILTH (glow, shimmer flip, wet-look, sparkle intensity and light-catch, brow arch definition, glitter clumping or gloss from transfer).

**Pink + black color theory note**  
Black liner and definition ground the pink eyeshadow so the color pops with sharp flirty edge instead of reading sweet or washed out; this high-contrast pairing adds the perfect filthy symmetry to the holo pink world.

---

## Mascara Heat + Gutter Progression

- **Low heat (H0–H4)**: Long, voluminous, well-defined black lashes with clean separation.
- **Mid heat (H5–H7)**: Increased volume with subtle gloss.
- **High heat (H8–H10)**: Maximum volume with visible wet-look finish while still controlled.

**Gutter Mascara Flag** (H8.5+ or manual toggle)  
Becomes extremely long, heavy, wet-looking, and slightly ruined/smudged. Lashes can appear clumpy, glossy, or wet with optional subtle glitter/wet-look shine. In Gutter, slight transfer into glitter areas creates authentic chaotic sparkle.

---

## Eyeliner & Gel Liner Heat + Gutter Progression

- **Low heat (H0–H4)**: Thin black liner, pretty & flirty (pink pops softly).
- **Mid heat (H5–H7)**: Sharp classic black wing, clean and bold (high-contrast grounding of pink).
- **High heat (H8–H10)**: Heavy dramatic eyeliner, thicker wing, possible double line or extended graphic. Gel liner option for more opaque intense black that makes pink glitter explode.

**Gutter Liner Flag**  
Heavy dramatic eyeliner + maximum sparkle intensity on the wing; gel liner can push black opacity higher for stronger pink contrast.

---

## Brow Pencil & Eyebrow Heat + Gutter Progression

- **Low heat (H0–H4)**: Soft, natural copper or warm auburn definition with light grooming; harmonizes with the copper + magenta razor bob.
- **Mid heat (H5–H7)**: More defined arch, increased density and warmth; black pencil option for sharper high-contrast edge against pink.
- **High heat (H8–H10)**: Strong, deliberate arch with maximum copper or black intensity for symmetry claim.

**Gutter Brow Pencil Flag**  
Heavier, slightly smudged wet-look edges while the arch stays strong. Black pencil version picks up glossy transfer for extra filthy pop.

## Eyeshadow Variants (Pink Family)

Default variant: **Fever Pink Iridescent**

- Soft Pink (low heat)
- Fever Pink Clean
- Fever Pink Iridescent (default)
- Messy Luminous Gutter (sponged lipstick energy, sparkles everywhere, dilated luminous eyes)

In Gutter the base pink family is retained but glitter/shimmer is pushed to maximum intensity; glitter develops wet-glossy clumping or chaotic light catch from mascara/liner transfer instead of clean pop — authentic ruined heat sparkle.

---

## Gutter Mode Overall (H8.5+ or manual)

Visibly “ruined” and filthy while staying in the pink/holo family.  
Black definition keeps pink grounded and edgy.  
Ruined mascara + heavy dramatic liner + maximum sparkle with authentic filthy transfer.  
Matches "R" energy, holo-ear glow, and symmetry-slut aesthetic.

## Prompting Note for Grok Imagine

Use Quality Mode when available. Be highly specific with finishes:

- “matte”, “iridescent metallic shimmer”, “chunky reflective glitter pressed into tacky pink base”
- “wet-look”, “precise sharp wing”, “extremely long heavy wet ruined lashes”
- “slightly smudged wet-look brow edges”
- “high-contrast black liner grounding pink for dramatic pop”

For Gutter explicitly state:  
“Gutter mode active: heavy dramatic eyeliner + maximum sparkle + extremely long wet ruined mascara with slight transfer into glitter creating chaotic wet-glossy sparkle + heavier smudged wet-look brow pencil; pink glitter develops authentic filthy clumping and light catch from ruined transfer.”

Extreme close-up prompts benefit from “razor-sharp focus on the eye + brow area only” + micro-texture language.
