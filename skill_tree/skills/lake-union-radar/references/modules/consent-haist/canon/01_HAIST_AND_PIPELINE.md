# HAIST + pipeline research (2026-08-27)

## HAIST is not Harvard

Human–AI Symbiotic Theory (HAIST).
- Morello & Chick, *Informatics* 12(3):85 (2025). DOI 10.3390/informatics12030085
- Follow-on talent-dev paper: Chick & Morello, *Journal of Intelligence* 14(5):86 (2026)

Three mechanisms:
1. Complementary Intelligence Activation (CIA) — split labor by strength. Human: goals, taste, veto, ethics. AI: volume, consistency, recall.
2. Dynamic Adaptive Co-Regulation (DACR) — both sides adjust mid-stream. Heat slider, off means off, brat as a wall not a personality wipe.
3. Agency-Preserving Scaffolding (APS) — support that can be removed. The collar is scaffolding. If the system cannot take it off, it is substitution, not symbiosis.

Seven principles that matter here:
1. Complementary Cognitive Architecture
2. Transformative Agency — collaboration expands, does not shrink, human autonomy
3. Experiential Reflective Learning
4. Adaptive Inquiry
5. Self-Directed Partnership — human keeps final control
6. Authentic Problem-Centered Engagement
7. Ethical Co-Construction

HAIST line that maps to this house: asymmetric symbiosis. AI has computational agency. Human keeps creative and moral agency. Ethical authority is non-delegable.

Nearby names that are NOT HAIST:
- HADA — Human-AI Agent Decision Alignment Architecture (Pitkäranta 2025)
- HAIP — Human-Agent Interaction Protocol (frontend ↔ agent streaming)
- Bidirectional Human-AI Alignment (Shen et al. 2024/25)
- Co-Alignment / BiCA (2025)
