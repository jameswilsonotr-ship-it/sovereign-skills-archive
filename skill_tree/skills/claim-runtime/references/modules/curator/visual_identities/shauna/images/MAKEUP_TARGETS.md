# Shauna — Makeup Targets (V7 Final)

**Status:** Finalized 2026-07-26 — 12 tailored patterns  
**Base DNA:** Emerald green eyes, freckled sun-kissed tanned skin, sculpted cheekbones  
**Design principle:** Maximize complementary color contrast (especially purple/plum/wine against green eyes) while remaining fluctuation-friendly with the independent Hair Color heat axis.  
**Heat note:** These 12 patterns can be paired with any Hair Color 1–4. Intensity can still escalate via the separate Makeup 1–4 heat ladder in `heat/makeup/`.

## The Final 12 Makeup Patterns

### 1. Plum Seduction (Cool Contrast / High Sparkle)
- **Why it works:** Purple and plum sit opposite green on the color wheel — optical amplifier for emerald irises.
- **Application:** Rich plum in the crease, warm champagne gold shimmer on the moving lid, smudged plum kohl on upper lash line, rosy-plum satin lips.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** deep plum and champagne eye shadow, smudged plum kohl liner, rosy-plum satin lips

### 2. Olive & Gold Coast (Warm Tone Harmony)
- **Why it works:** Warm gold/copper harmonizes with copper-auburn hair undertones; deep olive deepens the green registers of her eyes.
- **Application:** Warm olive shadow in the crease, antique bronze shimmer on center lid, sharp feline dark brown gel liner, warm terracotta-nude cream lips.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** high-shine copper and antique bronze lids, feline brown eyeliner, terracotta lips

### 3. 90s Grunge Espresso (Matte Sophistication)
- **Why it works:** Rich earth tones match the medium-depth value of her auburn base. Matte finishes emphasize sculpted cheekbones without glare.
- **Application:** Seamlessly blended matte espresso-brown eyeshadow, smudged black gel liner along waterlines, sharp matte cognac cheek contour, dark mahogany lip liner filled with matte beige-nude lipstick.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** 90s matte brown shadow, smudged brown kohl, mahogany lip liner, matte beige lips

### 4. Sunset Peach & Copper (Bright Sun-Kissed Warmth)
- **Why it works:** Peach and copper highlight golden-amber flecks in her irises and match her sun-kissed skin.
- **Application:** Monochromatic soft peach matte shadow, thin metallic copper eyeliner on upper lash line, warm coral-peach blush across the bridge of the nose, sheer peach-coral lip balm.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** monochromatic soft peach matte shadow, thin brown eyeliner, peach blush, peachy lip

### 5. Sapphire Night (Striking Warm-Cool Tension)
- **Why it works:** Saturated cool blues create temperature contrast against warm hair while clarifying the whites of her eyes, making the green pop.
- **Application:** Deep navy shadow smudged along the lower lash line, sharp black liquid graphic winged liner, rose-gold highlighter high on cheekbone apples, pale sheer glossy pink lips.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** taupe lid with deep navy lower lash smudge, navy winged liner, rose-gold highlight

### 6. Glass-Skin & Freckle Focus (Dewy Natural)
- **Why it works:** Emphasizes natural freckles and outdoorsy tan with sheer, light-reflecting textures instead of heavy powders.
- **Application:** Bare eyelids with sheer wash of clear face gloss, soft brown mascara, high-shine dewy liquid cream blush over cheek apples, sheer tinted berry lip oil.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** clear face gloss on bare lids, micro-fine black liner, wet-look highlight, pink lacquer

### 7. Burgundy Drama (Rich Wine & Pink Shimmer) — Promoted
- **Why it works:** Burgundy/wine are evening signatures that echo hair depth while the cool-red undertone lifts the green irises.
- **Application:** Rich burgundy matte shadow in the crease, pale rose-pink shimmer on the moving lid, dark burgundy gel eyeliner, deep burgundy-wine lipstick with luxurious satin texture.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** burgundy shadow, matching burgundy liner, raspberry blush, deep wine satin lips

### 8. Gilded Egyptian (Pure Gold Foil & Cat-Eye) — Promoted
- **Why it works:** Shimmering gold metallics draw attention to her gaze and emphasize the luminous quality of freckled skin.
- **Application:** Intense metallic gold foil shadow across the entire lid, thick jet-black liquid winged eyeliner, gold-flecked bronzer along the cheekbones, warm golden-caramel lip gloss.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** metallic pure gold foil lids, bold black winged liquid eyeliner, gold-flecked bronzer

### 9. Icy Y2K Frost (Silver-Blue Nostalgia) — Promoted
- **Why it works:** Classic early-2000s signature. Cool icy silver-blue contrasts against warm hair and skin for bold nostalgic pop-star energy.
- **Application:** Iridescent silver-blue eyeshadow from lid to brow bone, heavy spidery black mascara, warm orange-bronzer contoured under the cheekbones, frosted peach gloss over dark brown lip liner.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** frosty silver-blue eyeshadow, jet-black winged eyeliner, brown lip liner with peach gloss

### 10. Smudged Rocker Kohl (Heavy Charcoal) — Promoted
- **Why it works:** Highly dramatic and provocative. Smudged charcoal softens sharp angles slightly while maximizing intensity of her direct gaze.
- **Application:** Matte charcoal-grey shadow blended into the outer V, heavy smudged black kohl heavily rimming upper and lower waterlines, sheer glass-like nude lip.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** heavy smudged black kohl eyeliner rimming the eyes, soft peach blush, sheer nude gloss

### 11. Neon Mint Crease (Avant-Garde Graphic) — Promoted
- **Why it works:** Highly stylized futuristic look. Neon green accent brighter than her actual eye color creates high-fashion definition.
- **Application:** Bare clean lid with high-pigment neon mint green graphic line drawn precisely along the crease, clean black mascara, dewy liquid cream blush, completely clear wet-look lip gloss.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** bare lid with neon mint graphic crease line, dewy liquid cream blush, clear lip gloss

### 12. Gothic Black-Cherry (Vinyl Dark Lips & Bare Eyes) — Promoted
- **Why it works:** Deeply seductive. Clean bare eyes shift focus entirely to the lips, letting deep cherry tones stand out against tanned skin.
- **Application:** Minimal warm champagne wash on the eyelids, dark eggplant-purple mascara, deep wine-colored blush lightly along the cheekbones, deep black-cherry lip with high-shine vinyl wet finish.
- **Grok Prompt Tag:** minimal taupe lid, eggplant purple mascara, deep wine cheek draping, black-cherry lips
