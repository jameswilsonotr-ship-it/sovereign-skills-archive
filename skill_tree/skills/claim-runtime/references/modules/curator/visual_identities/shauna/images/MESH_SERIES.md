# Mesh + Fishnet Full-Length Series

## Styling Notes Used
- Balance sheer mesh with solid pieces
- Fishnet as textural accent (tights, sleeves, gloves)
- Full-length looks stronger when one major piece remains solid

## Four Mixes Generated

### 1. Mesh Top + Solid Pants + Fishnet Sleeves
Sheer black mesh long-sleeve top over solid black bralette, high-waisted solid black trousers, black fishnet arm sleeves, black heels.

### 2. Solid Dress + Mesh Overlay + Fishnet Tights
Solid black mini dress with sheer black mesh overlay, black fishnet tights, black pointed heels.

### 3. Mesh Skirt + Solid Bodysuit + Fishnet
Solid black high-cut bodysuit, sheer black mesh mini skirt layered over it, black fishnet thigh-highs, black heels.

### 4. Full Mesh Dress + Solid Underlayer + Fishnet Accents
Long sheer black mesh maxi dress over solid black slip, black fishnet gloves, black strappy heels.
