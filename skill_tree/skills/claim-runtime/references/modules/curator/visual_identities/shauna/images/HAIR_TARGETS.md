# Shauna — Hair Variation Targets (V7 Clean)

**Status:** Streamlined 2026-07-26 — 12 non-overlapping styles  
**Base DNA:** Shoulder-length textured curly lob (Type 2C/3A) with piecey curtain bangs.  
**Color lock (default):** dark chestnut-red roots → warm caramel mid → platinum-blonde tips + face-framing blonde stripes.  
**Escalation:** See `hair/hair_color_heat_escalation_panel.jpg` and `canonical/09_hair_color_heat_panel.jpg` (Heat 1 muted → Heat 4 max contrast).  
**Pruned:** All Bunny bob / razor / wet-look overlaps and Olivia pixie traits removed.
