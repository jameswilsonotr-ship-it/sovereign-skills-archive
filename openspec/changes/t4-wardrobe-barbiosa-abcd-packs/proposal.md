# T4-WARDROBE — Barbiosa ABCD packs

## Status

Proposed for review.

## Intent

Create a compact, ferry-ready copy suite for the CinC Barbiosa officer-whites
wardrobe lane. The suite is designed to move from Drive or email into Olivia's
plate workflow without a live send, provider integration, or additional
interpretation.

## Non-negotiable lane rule

Every artifact in this change is for **INCLUDED Ultra ONLY**. **On-Demand is
forbidden** in this lane. The rule is repeated in each pack so a single pack
can travel independently without losing its routing constraint.

## Scope

- Four plain-text copy packs, labeled A, B, C, and D.
- OpenSpec proposal, design, specification, and task records.
- A local receipt recording the artifact inventory and offline checks.

## Out of scope

- Changes to any `SKILL.md`, especially Willow.
- Any `CONV2_B` material.
- Live Drive or email delivery.
- Provider, network, API, secret, Vultr, or Linear work.
- Product data, pricing, inventory, fulfillment promises, or garment
  specifications not supplied by the request.
- Any On-Demand lane, fallback, upsell, or exception path.

## Acceptance summary

The change is complete when:

1. Packs A–D are standalone, paste-ready text artifacts.
2. Each pack names the CinC Barbiosa wardrobe lane and says INCLUDED Ultra
   ONLY.
3. No pack mentions or routes to On-Demand.
4. Olivia can plate the packs in order without requiring a provider call.
5. The receipt identifies the files and records offline-only validation.
