# Design — CinC Barbiosa officer-whites lane

## Content shape

The four packs form one small handoff, but each is independently ferry-ready:

| Pack | Job | Olivia plate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Set the scene and lane | Cover / hero |
| B | Name the wardrobe beats | Look or item tiles |
| C | Supply the finishing language | Detail / caption strip |
| D | Close the handoff cleanly | Footer / email body |

The copy uses short labels, generous line breaks, and a single visual idea per
section. Olivia can lift a complete block or split it at the labeled dividers.

## Routing guardrails

- The lane label is exactly `CINC BARBIOSA · OFFICER WHITES` in prose as
  `CinC Barbiosa · officer whites`; capitalization may be styled by Olivia.
- Every pack carries the routing stamp `INCLUDED ULTRA ONLY`.
- The forbidden fulfillment phrase is not used in any pack.
- No prices, stock claims, links, recipient addresses, or send instructions
  are invented.
- The copy says what the wardrobe lane is, not what a provider has done.

## Tone

Bright, polished, and lightly playful: white-on-white clarity, command
presence, and a small wink of ceremony. “Cute” is expressed through rhythm,
em-dashes, and restrained sparkle rather than claims about fit, material, or
availability.

## Handoff behavior

The text packs are repository artifacts only. A human may copy them into a
Drive or email workflow and Olivia may plate them. This change does not send,
upload, call, authenticate, or connect to any external service.
